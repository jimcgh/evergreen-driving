Class 5 Road Test Prep

Elevate your driving skills and eliminate bad habits to pass your full-privilege ICBC Class 5 road test with confidence.

Breaking Bad Habits

Experienced drivers often develop habits that ICBC examiners penalize. We identify and correct rolling stops, one-handed steering, and missed shoulder checks.

Highway & Complex Merging

The Class 5 test may include highway driving. We ensure you can confidently accelerate, merge smoothly, and maintain appropriate following distances at high speeds.
