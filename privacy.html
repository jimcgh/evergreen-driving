Privacy Policy

Last updated:

1. Introduction

At Evergreen Driving School, we are committed to maintaining the trust and confidence of our visitors to our web site. This Privacy Policy outlines how we collect, use, and protect your personal information in compliance with the Personal Information Protection and Electronic Documents Act (PIPEDA) of Canada and other applicable privacy regulations.

2. Information We Collect

We may collect two types of information when you visit our website:

  • Personal Information: Such as your name, email address, and phone number, which you voluntarily provide to us via our contact forms. This information is solely used to respond to your inquiries and arrange driving lessons.
  • Anonymized Usage Data: We use Google Analytics (GA4) to collect anonymized data about how you interact with our website (e.g., pages visited, duration of visit, and device types). This data does not identify you personally.

3. How We Use Cookies

Cookies are small text files stored on your device. We use cookies to enhance your browsing experience and analyze site traffic through Google Analytics. You can choose to accept or decline cookies via our cookie consent banner. You can also configure your browser settings to block or delete cookies at any time.

4. Third-Party Services (Google Analytics)

Our website uses Google Analytics (GA4) to help us analyze website traffic. Google Analytics may store cookies on your device to gather anonymized information. Google's ability to use and share information collected by Google Analytics about your visits to this site is restricted by the Google Analytics Terms of Use and the Google Privacy Policy.

5. Information Sharing & Protection

We do not sell, trade, or otherwise transfer your personal information to outside parties. We implement appropriate technical and organizational security measures to protect your personal data from unauthorized access, alteration, or disclosure.

6. Your Rights

Under PIPEDA, you have the right to access the personal information we hold about you, request corrections to any inaccuracies, or ask us to delete your data. If you have any questions or wish to exercise these rights, please contact us using the information below.
